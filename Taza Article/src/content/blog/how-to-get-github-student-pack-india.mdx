## 2. Step-by-Step GitHub Student Pack Application

### Step 1: Prepare Your Verification Proof
If your college has issued you an institutional email ending in `.ac.in`, `.edu`, or your college's official domain name, you are in luck. Applications with institutional emails are often approved within minutes.

**What if your college does not provide a student email?**
Don't worry! GitHub officially accepts physical ID cards and bonafide student certificates.
Make sure your ID card clearly shows:
1. Your full name (must match your GitHub billing profile name).
2. The official name and logo of your college.
3. The validity date or current academic year (e.g. 2024-2028 or Valid Upto: June 2026).

### Step 2: Enable Location Services & Two-Factor Authentication
GitHub checks your browser geolocation during the application to verify that you are physically in India near your university campus.
- Turn off any VPN or proxy services before applying.
- Enable 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your GitHub account settings.

### Step 3: Take a Live Photo of Your College ID
GitHub's automated verification system requires a live webcam photo rather than a pre-scanned upload:
1. Place your college ID card on a flat, well-lit surface.
2. Ensure there is no bright glare on your photograph or admission number.
3. Capture the live photo using your phone camera or laptop webcam.

## 3. Why Do Indian Students Get Rejected? (And How to Fix It)

If your application was rejected, review these three most common mistakes:

1. **Name Mismatch:** Your GitHub account username can be anything, but your **GitHub Billing Name** must match the exact spelling on your college ID.
2. **Expired or Blank Validity Date:** If your college ID does not print an explicit expiry year, obtain a signed **Bonafide Student Certificate** from your college administrative office on official college letterhead.
3. **VPN Usage:** Applying while connected to a corporate VPN or overseas proxy will trigger automated fraud filters.
